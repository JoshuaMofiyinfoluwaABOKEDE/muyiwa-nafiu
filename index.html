The book

Serving As A Psalmist

Spiritual and ethical counsel for the gospel musician.

Not a research paper, but “a chronicle of inspiration and experiences” from a sanctuary psalmist. Written for pastors, worship leaders, choir members, instrumentalists and sound teams, it is a book on maximising the saints’ community: staying planted in a local church, discovering your assignment, songwriting, fame, livelihood, and leading with a clear sense of who you are in the Father’s house.

“A book with a narrow title but a wide audience.”

Rev. Niyi Adebayo, Lead Pastor, True Worshippers Church, Ogbomoso, from the foreword

About Muyiwa

A minister in word, in deed, and in song.

Muyiwa Nafiu — born Nafiu Olumuyiwa Kolade — comes from Ileogbo, Osun State, lived in Minna from 2015 to 2022, and now lives and serves in Ogbomoso, Nigeria. He studied Anatomy at LAUTECH, Ogbomoso, but ministry has carried most of his attention for years.

He calls himself “a sanctuary psalmist by training and by practice.” He serves as Minister of Music at True Worshippers Church, Ogbomoso, under Reverend ’Niyi Adebayo, and from 2016 to 2022 he was a worship leader at Great House Ministries, Minna. Alongside ministry trips, he trains gospel music ministers and worship leaders through O’Timothy and the Worship Leaders Meeting. He is married to Folasade.

“Real ministry is a result of your compassion, and not of your need.”
